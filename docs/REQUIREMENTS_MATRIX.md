## Behaviour notes the tests pin down
- **403 vs 404 (intentional):** a user who is not in the item's team gets 404 for every item route, so the response reveals nothing about whether the item exists. A user who IS in the team but lacks the role gets 403.
- **Idempotency retention:** a key is remembered for 24 hours per (actor, operation). Within that window the same payload replays the original response and a different payload is 409. After it, the key is treated as new. There is no background purge; stale rows are removed when their key is reused.
- **Outbox delivery guarantee:** at-least-once. The queued job and the business change commit together, so a committed change is never lost; a job may be delivered more than once (crash after the side effect, lease expiry), so handlers must be idempotent. The notification sink is unique per job id. An external call (email, webhook) cannot be made exactly-once by a database transaction alone: pass the job id as the provider's idempotency key.
